How to use this guide

Unit 2 is about how Hadoop actually works: how it stores huge files across many machines (HDFS) and how it processes them in parallel (MapReduce, YARN). Read in order — Chapter 1 gives the big picture, Chapters 2 and 3 zoom into storage and processing.

💡 How to study the "step" topics
Unit 2 has many step-by-step processes (file read: 6 steps, file write: 7 steps, MR1 job run: 10 steps, YARN job run: 11 steps). For each one:
  1. First learn the actors (who is involved — client, NameNode, DataNode…).
  2. Then trace the diagram with your finger, saying each numbered arrow out loud.
  3. Then close the book and redraw the diagram from memory. If you can draw it, you can write the answer.
🎯 About CIE-1
CIE-1 (30 marks) covers CO1–CO3: Unit 1, Unit 2 and the first part of Unit 3. Unit 2 is the most diagram-heavy unit — "Anatomy of a file read/write", "Classic MapReduce vs YARN", "Shuffle and sort" and "HDFS architecture" are classic 10-mark questions. Always write: definition → diagram → numbered steps → example.
