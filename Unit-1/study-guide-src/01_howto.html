How to use this guide

This guide assumes you know nothing about Big Data yet. Read it in order: each chapter builds on the previous one. Everything from your Unit 1 slides (Lectures 1–4) is here, rewritten in simple language, with the textbook terms you need to write in the exam.

💡 How to study each section
  1. Read the "In simple words" box first — get the idea before the definition.
  2. Look at the diagram and try to explain it out loud in 2–3 lines.
  3. Learn the textbook definition (the boxed Definition) — examiners like the exact wording.
  4. At the end of every chapter, tick the "Can you…?" checklist. If you can't tick something, go back.
  5. The night before the exam, read only the Cheat Sheet (last pages) and the 🔑 boxes.
🎯 About CIE-1
CIE-1 is for 30 marks and covers CO1, CO2 & CO3 → Unit 1, Unit 2 and the first part of Unit 3 (Spark architecture overview). This guide covers Unit 1 completely. For long answers (5–10 marks), always write: definition → explanation in points → diagram → example. A neat diagram is often worth 1–2 marks by itself.
